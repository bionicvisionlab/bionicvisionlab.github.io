Research Areas

Artificial Vision Across the Visual Pathway

We study how stimulation of the retina, LGN, and visual cortex produces artificial vision, and which biological details matter most for predicting the resulting percepts.

Emily M. Joyce · Hannah L. Stone · Eirini Schoinas

Visual Prostheses Brain Stimulation Computational Neuroscience

NeuroAI & Visual Representations

We compare representations in biological and artificial visual systems to understand what makes them similar, where they differ, and which biological constraints matter for learning and behavior.

Lucas Nadolskis · Galen Pogoncheff

NeuroAI Computational Neuroscience Systems Neuroscience

Active Vision & Sensorimotor Intelligence

We study vision during natural behavior: how eye, head, and body movements shape the visual input, how the brain responds, and how biological and artificial systems solve similar sensorimotor problems.

Yuchen Hou · Marius Schneider

Active Vision Systems Neuroscience Computational Neuroscience
